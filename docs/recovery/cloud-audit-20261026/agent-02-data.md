## Real remaining gaps

1. **Depth primary feature recovery and downstream visibility:** canonical manifest explicitly reports `INTEGRITY_BLOCKED_PRIMARY_FEATURE_GAP`; local encrypted feature inventory confirms secondary/third only. Do not weaken integrity gates. Current Sentinel (2026-10-26 14:13:46Z) also reports failed depth-context/sweep workflows and D1 `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` (four days).
2. **Staged Yahoo corpus paths are stale for this workspace:** ingestion manifest says two Yahoo archives/local directories exist, but `/workspace/yahoo_corpus_1m_recent_2026-10-16/`, `/workspace/yahoo_corpus_1h_2026-10-16/` and matching local archives were not found. `SHARING_UNVERIFIED` is already explicit. This is local unavailability, not a claim about Dropbox.
3. **Sample corpora are not integrated:** real trial MBO/MBP bytes and separate symbol CSV lack an identified Icarus consumer. Either ingest with reviewed provenance/semantics or label them reference-only. Do not call them operational data.
4. **Extraction does not establish CSV intake:** configured ZIP discovery/extraction is present, but no local extracted history/receipt was established by this audit. Keep ingestion and qualified downstream use separate.
5. **Freshness/entitlement remain bounded:** EIA is a dated static panel; news endpoints include explicit plan denial; historical provider/consumption receipts cannot certify current access. Sentinel has 37 failing, six blocked, five degraded, two stale sources. Many Databento bar failures are labeled `root not in CL_DATABENTO_ROOTS`; diagnose configuration/health scope before claiming vendor-data corruption.

No live execution authority was inferred. These findings are audit handoffs, not assertions that all local data has passed semantic validation.
