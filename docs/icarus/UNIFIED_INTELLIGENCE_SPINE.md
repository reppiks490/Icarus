# ICARUS Unified Intelligence Spine

The Unified Intelligence Spine is the composition boundary that turns ICARUS from a federation of advanced subsystems into one inspectable research organism without erasing subsystem identity.

## Non-negotiable contracts

1. Every contribution keeps its source identity.
2. Causal availability requires both observation time and receipt time to be available by the requested as-of boundary.
3. Shared ancestry is discounted instead of being counted as independent confirmation.
4. Disagreement is preserved rather than averaged away invisibly.
5. Foreign lenses remain quarantined and removable.
6. Structural novelty is not called predictive information gain.
7. Predictive promotion remains downstream of protected ICARUS validation.
8. The spine has no production-decision or execution authority.

## Canonical claim

Each contributor emits a source-scoped claim with:

- source and source kind;
- canonical semantic key;
- signed stance in [-1,+1];
- confidence in [0,1];
- evidence class;
- observed and received timestamps;
- regime and horizon;
- evidence-lineage roots;
- explicit falsifiers;
- finite JSON details.

The claim receives a deterministic content identity.

## Fusion

Claims are grouped by semantic key, regime, and horizon. Within a group, evidence sharing the same lineage contributes at most once; the strongest-confidence representative of that lineage family is retained for the aggregate. The fused state exposes consensus stance, weighted disagreement, raw claim count, effective independent evidence families, shared-ancestry discount, directional conflict, source list, and evidence classes.

The original claims are never overwritten.

## Contribution accounting

For every source the spine reports structural novelty and structural redundancy relative to all other current sources. These are representation-overlap diagnostics only.

`predictive_incremental_information` remains `null` until a protected outcome-validation process measures it. This is deliberate: ICARUS must not confuse a new representation with an edge.

## Foreign perception systems

"The Eyes Through F4D3" and any later external methodology enter as `source_kind=foreign_lens`. They can contribute hypotheses and semantic state, but remain quarantined. Their unique contribution can therefore be measured, ablated, falsified, retired, or promoted through normal ICARUS candidate qualification without contaminating native subsystem identity.

## Intended next integration

The next runtime stage should adapt native outputs from Chronofold, Ψ, APEX, PANTHEON, SIBYL, ARGUS/NEXUS, and qualified foreign lenses into this canonical claim contract, then persist fused snapshots and route them into PARALLAX/DAEDALUS for outcome-based incremental-value testing.

That downstream test is the actual standard:

```
Does source S improve protected out-of-sample outcome quality
conditional on the rest of ICARUS?
```

If not, the source stays redundant or is retired. If yes, the surviving semantic contribution can advance through the existing protected gate stack.
