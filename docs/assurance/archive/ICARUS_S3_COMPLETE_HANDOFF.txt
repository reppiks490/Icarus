# 8. ESTIMATOR-VALIDITY GATE

Allowed classifications:
ESTIMATOR_VALIDITY_STATUS =
  VALIDATED | PARTIAL | HEURISTIC_ONLY | CONTRADICTED | UNKNOWN

When an ICARUS feature is used as evidence for a latent property such as:
- persistence;
- mean reversion;
- regime;
- entropy;
- fractality;
- predictability;
- volatility state;
- correlation structure;

test whether the estimator actually identifies that property under realistic financial time-series
conditions.

Check:
- finite-sample bias;
- short-memory contamination;
- heteroskedasticity;
- structural breaks;
- nonstationarity;
- overlapping-window dependence;
- estimator/window sensitivity.

Require appropriate null/synthetic controls for estimator validation (not as empirical market
evidence) and at least one conceptually independent estimator/diagnostic when the latent-property
claim materially affects promotion.

HURST SPECIAL RULE:
Classical R/S Hurst alone must never authorize trend or mean-reversion claims. Compare against a
short-memory-robust or frequency-domain alternative and ablate Hurst before granting authority.

independence between correlated votes.

For material confluence families require, when feasible:
- train-only pairwise association;
- nonlinear dependence such as MI/HSIC;
- minimum-redundancy/relevance analysis;
- leave-one-vote-out ablation;
- leave-one-family-out ablation;
- conditional/incremental predictive value against a simpler baseline;
- block/bootstrap or time-respecting permutation rather than IID shuffling.

Track OOS changes in:
- net-after-costs;
- drawdown/tails;
- calibration;
- turnover;
- regime stability.

Predictive alone + no conditional OOS value => REDUNDANT.

Improved in-sample fit + no held-out improvement => REJECTED or UNRESOLVED.

Do not reward correlated evidence repeatedly through raw vote count, family bonus, and adaptive
weighting unless dependence correction is explicitly validated OOS.
